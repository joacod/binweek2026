# The Hive Reunion artwork

Original game illustrations generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-13. Editable PNGs live in `sources/`; exact production prompts and tool provenance live in [prompts.json](prompts.json). Original artwork is distributed under the repository's [MIT license](../../../LICENSE).

The official `binagora-logo.png` (474×132) and `binagora-icon.png` (192×192) are faithful, unmodified company marks downloaded from [Binagora's logo](https://binagora.com/images/logo.png) and [icon](https://binagora.com/favicons/android-icon-192x192.png). They remain Binagora's property, packaged for this company event game at the user's direction, and are excluded from the original-art MIT grant. Do not reuse them as a generic art pack. No third-party marketing photography is included in the game.

The hotel illustration references the [official hotel](https://www.hermitagehotel.com.ar/) and [MPMC exterior gallery](https://mpmcarquitectos.com/hotel-hermitage/), including its [facade photograph](https://mpmcarquitectos.com/wp-content/gallery/hotel-hermitage/Hermitage-02.jpg). The illustrated hotel preserves the stepped three-part massing, red walls, cream trim, slate mansard roofs, pale base and one readable hotel sign. Coastal plates interpret El Puerto, Playa Grande, Cabo Corrientes/Varese, Torreón del Monje and the Rambla as a stylized montage. The central plate prominently interprets the pair of [Fioravanti sea-lion monuments on the Rambla](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/escultura-lobos-marinos), kept exclusively as scenery. Rabas replace the collectible souvenir. The official [Argentina tourism food guide](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/jefatura/turismo/viaja-por-argentina/comer-pescados-y-frutos-de-mar-en-el-puerto) identifies rabas among the port’s seafood dishes. The municipal [coastal beach guide](https://www.turismomardelplata.gob.ar/ASP/SP/detalles-playas.asp?Cod_Lugar=24739) distinguishes Playa Grande’s surf from the sheltered Varese/Cabo Corrientes bays, while its [audio tours](https://www.turismomardelplata.gob.ar/asp/sp/audioguias.htm) describe the fishing port and traditional seaside neighborhoods. These inform a stylized south-to-center route, not a scale map. Food references imply no sponsorship.

The detailed charcoal bee wears amber sunglasses and a travel satchel, with textured fur, articulated legs and veined translucent wings. It uses a 94-unit square anchored at 55% across and 60% down. Wing animation switches complete cels, with a brief delighted cel on collection. The body/bag pickup envelope is owned by `content.ts`, independently of the forgiving 16-unit hazard radius. Food images draw at 40 units with the same 13×13-unit diamond collection envelope. The gull draws at 116 units around its unchanged 35-unit collision radius. Its runtime body anchor is (256, 280) in each 512-pixel cel. Complete wings, beak and stolen churro stay in the padded cel. A bounded upstroke/glide/downstroke sequence includes a gliding rest; reduced motion uses a steady glide. There is no danger halo.

Landmarks never tile or mirror. Each panorama fits across the viewport, with its quiet top sky extended upwards on tall screens so the coastline and both monuments remain visible. A monotonic exponential pan consumes at most 28 percent of viewport width from a 128-percent-wide plate. It never wraps, reverses or resets at a checkpoint. Clouds, foam and whole shoreline props travel at increasing depths; a fishing boat sits on water rather than the promenade. Reduced motion freezes these layers. Chapter dissolves use elapsed simulation time relative to the saved checkpoint, so pause freezes them and continuation restores the outgoing scene. The final checkpoint retains the Rambla. Coastal laptop vignettes pass using saved distance and dissolve with their place, including the Lobo Language Model and the illustrated LGTM reviewer, without entering simulation state.

Solid promenade piers fill the actual top/bottom collision regions. Their painted irregular quartzite, pale stepped cornices and terracotta flight edges interpret the city's coastal stonework. The municipal [city guide](https://www.turismomardelplata.gob.ar/asp/sp/la-ciudad.htm) identifies local quartzite as “piedra Mar del Plata”, and the national [Chalet Ave María heritage entry](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/capital-humano/cultura/monumentos/chalet-ave-maria) describes the local architectural use of stone and natural materials. These are material references, not replicas of individual landmarks. Promenade supports suit the rectangular flight hazards better than pitched-roof chalets or recognizable towers hanging upside down. The original gray block grid and industrial warning stripes are replaced by one painted material with Canvas-owned moldings. The material repeats at a fixed 128-world-unit size, anchored to each pier without stretching as openings move. Cornices stay inside the solid collision rectangles. Event beacons are Canvas-authored orange hash badges with a one-time activation bloom. Crowd members repeat the complete bee cels with bounded pose, direction, cup and scarf variants. The complete illustrated reviewer and the Canvas-owned captions and confetti have no simulation rules.

## Repacking

Run `pnpm art:pack`. The 1254×1254 bee source resizes as a whole sheet to 1024×1024, preserving alpha and fixed cell boundaries. Do not independently trim bee frames. The original `sources/objects.png` remains the authoring input for the complete alfajor and churros cels. The top-left quadrant of `sources/snacks-gull.png` supplies rabas. These food cells are contained in 448×448 areas with 32-pixel padding.

The other three quadrants of `snacks-gull.png` each contain an entire bird. Explicit source body pivots in `pack.mjs` align them at (256, 280) after a uniform 401/627 resize; do not independently fit birds to their wing bounds. The source sheet remains untouched. `scenery.png` resizes as a whole sheet to 1024×1024. `reviewer.png` is contained in a 768×384 transparent frame. Scenery/reviewer use high-quality WebP with full-quality alpha; bird and food use lossless WebP. All five coastal plates and the hotel encode at 1536×1024.

`sources/quartzite.png` is an original opaque material illustration generated with the built-in image tool using `sources/beach.png` as a style reference. It resizes as a whole to 512×512 high-quality WebP. The source and exact prompt are retained alongside the other original artwork under MIT.

Original PNGs and exact production prompts remain authoring inputs and do not enter the browser build. `art.ts` validates decoded dimensions before play; `presentation.ts` owns footprints, cells and pivots. All runtime paths are static and relative.
