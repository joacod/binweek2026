# Binagora Week 2026 — The Hive Reunion

“The hive is gathering at the Hermitage. See you there!” A short continuous coastal flight carries Binagora's bee from El Puerto through Playa Grande and Torreón del Monje to the Rambla and Hermitage Hotel. Five checkpoint legs take about two minutes of successful active flight. The three visual chapters follow the player's passed checkpoints (0–1, 2–3, 4), never the generator's lookahead. The final clear-air checkpoint ends the run once, regardless of collection score.

## Flight and recovery

Hold Space, W, ↑, mouse/touch on the playfield, or Rise to ascend. Release to descend. A/D, ←/→, touch arrows or sideways drag slide inside the fixed scrolling view. The launch press also applies thrust. Flight acceleration, braking, speed milestones, lateral motion, narrow-screen warning time and three lives retain the extracted game's behavior. The edges cushion contact. Solid structures and gulls cost one life per unshielded crash.

The seeded route retains actual-physics clearance certification, shared moving-obstacle positions and safe collectible arcs. Later coastal openings are deliberately wider (230, 220, 214 and 210 units before moving-opening adjustments) than the endless game's late challenges. There is no generation beyond the fifth checkpoint. A final clear-air approach reveals the hotel before arrival.

Event hash beacons activate once: “Checkpoint committed.” Continue restores route distance, score, pickup streak, shield, difficulty and obstacle clock from the latest in-memory checkpoint, centered and still in clear air. Lives do not refill and rewards cannot be farmed across retries. Restart makes a fresh route with three lives. No browser storage, saved results, personal bests or export features are used.

## Collectibles and collision

Alfajores are the recurring reward, with churros and miniature stone sea-lion souvenirs as occasional appearances. All share one score and shield path. Distance awards one point per displayed meter. Collectibles start at 25 points; five consecutive pickups raise subsequent values through ×2, ×3 and ×4. Missing a reward behind the rear flight slot resets the streak once. Cosmetic missed rewards remain until the viewport clips them.

The bee deliberately replaces the rider/scooter pickup hull with a body-and-bag octagon spanning ±25 units horizontally and −23 to +25 vertically. Wings and antennae are decorative. The forgiving 16-unit circular hazard body is unchanged. Rewards use a 13-by-13 half-size diamond envelope, replacing the narrow crystal's 10-by-15 envelope. Swept collection still catches fast travel and lateral crossings. Stone fills the actual top/bottom collision regions, and the gull uses the original moving hazard's 35-unit radius and position.

Thirty collectibles charge a five-second Hive Shield. Its bubble and meter show live protection. One hit breaks it immediately, allowing that contact to finish safely. Another obstacle or contact after separation is dangerous. Unused protection expires, charge carries across misses, and checkpoint continuation restores only its saved state.
