## Flight and recovery

Hold Space, W, ↑, mouse/touch on the playfield, or Rise to ascend. Release to descend. A/D, ←/→, touch arrows or sideways drag slide inside the fixed scrolling view. Mouse and keyboard launch presses also apply thrust. Touch launch waits for a completed tap so mobile browsers can activate audio before flight. Flight acceleration, braking, speed milestones, lateral motion, narrow-screen warning time and three lives retain the extracted game's behavior. The edges cushion contact. Solid structures and gulls cost one life per unshielded crash.

The seeded route retains actual-physics clearance certification, shared moving-obstacle positions and safe collectible arcs. Later coastal openings are deliberately wider (230, 220, 214 and 210 units before moving-opening adjustments) than the endless game's late challenges. There is no generation beyond the fifth checkpoint. A final clear-air approach reveals the hotel before arrival.

Event hash beacons activate once: “Checkpoint committed.” Continue restores route distance, score, pickup streak, shield, difficulty and obstacle clock from the latest in-memory checkpoint, centered and still in clear air. Lives do not refill and rewards cannot be farmed across retries. Restart makes a fresh route with three lives. No browser storage, saved results, personal bests or export features are used.
